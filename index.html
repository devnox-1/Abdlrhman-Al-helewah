Growth Framework

How I Approach Performance Growth.

A data-driven, risk-managed operating system designed for scalable customer acquisition and high-ticket lead generation. Grounded in statistical thresholds, margin protection, and creative velocity.

Controlled Scaling

Conversion Volume & Scaling Velocity

Budget is scaled incrementally (+15%–20% every 48–72 hours) only when conversion volume is statistically stable and verified ROAS/CPA holds above target. Prevents algorithmic learning reset and CPM spikes.

Condition: Sustained baseline across conversion lag window
Margin Protection

Disciplined Stop-Loss (1.5x CPA)

Zero emotional attachment to creative angles. Any ad set reaching 1.5x of target CPA without generating verified purchases or leads is sunset immediately to protect margin and cash flow.

Condition: Spend ≥ 1.5x Target CPA with 0 conversions
Creative Velocity

Fatigue Monitoring & Proactive Refresh

Tracking 3-second Hook Rate and Outbound CTR daily. When creative engagement dips >20% below benchmark, fresh hook variants are deployed before CPA degradation occurs.
